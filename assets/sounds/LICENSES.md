# Sounds

Four cat sounds, cut from recordings on Wikimedia Commons that their
authors released as CC0 or into the public domain. No credit is owed;
the sources are named so anyone can hear the originals. Every cut is
mono, 22.05 kHz, faded, and loudness-matched with ffmpeg.

## tick.wav — a chore ticked

"Maullido de gata hembra joven" by George Miquilena, CC0,
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maullido_de_gata_hembra_joven.ogg
The first 0.8 seconds.

## purr.wav — a day of chores done

"Whiskers' purr" by Adam Cuerden, public domain,
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Whiskers%27_purr.ogg
Seconds 1 to 3.8.

## chorus.wav — a ladder climbed

Three meows one after the other:
"2015-11-24.νιαούρισμα.Νιάου.noise reduced" by Tsester, CC0,
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2015-11-24.νιαούρισμα.Νιάου.noise_reduced.flac
"Meow of a Siamese cat - freemaster2" by freemaster2, CC0,
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meow_of_a_Siamese_cat_-_freemaster2.wav
and "Maullido de gata hembra joven" as above.

## party.wav — an adoption

"Purring cat bertie" by jim_mowatt, public domain,

under the Siamese meow above.
