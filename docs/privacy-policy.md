# cat(a)log — Privacy Policy

cat(a)log stores all data — cats, places, photos, positions — locally on
your device. There is no server, no account, and no analytics. Nothing is
collected, transmitted to, or stored by the developer or any third party.

Data leaves your device only when you explicitly trigger it: syncing
directly with another device you chose, writing to a shared folder you
chose, or sharing a card or CSV export through your platform's share
sheet. Map tiles are fetched from OpenStreetMap when you open the map,
and when you search for a place or address in the position picker, on
the map when your catalog has no match for the search, or when you ask
the flier scan to find the owner's address, that search text is sent to
OpenStreetMap's Nominatim geocoding service; OpenStreetMap's privacy
policy applies to both (https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

Opening a registry number (for example a Tasso search number stored on
a cat) hands the link to your browser, which then contacts that service
under its own privacy policy. cat(a)log never queries a registry
itself.

Location access is used solely to record positions of cats when you tap
"Stray Cam" or "seen here now", and camera/photo access solely to attach
photos to cats. Both stay on your device like everything else.
